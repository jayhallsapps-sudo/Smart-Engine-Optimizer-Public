# SOP: SEO Bi-Weekly Meetings

**Purpose**: The Bi-Weekly SEO meeting exists to reinforce trust, maintain project momentum, and keep the client feeling seen, heard, and informed — without turning the call into a data dump. These sessions should feel consultative and forward-moving: "Here's what's happening, why it matters, and what's next."

---

## Meeting Specs

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Meeting Length | 30 minutes |
| Prep Time | ≤ 30 minutes |
| Responsible | SEO Account Manager |
| Informed | SEO Associate Director |
| Scheduling | Recurrent 3rd week of the month |

> Try and never schedule meetings on Fridays to protect your working time.

**Meeting Title**: [Client Name]: WebServ SEO Bi-Weekly Check-In

---

## Required Attendees

| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Account Manager (AM) | Leads the call, presents updates, manages agenda and relationship |
| Client POC / Marketing Contact | Main contact for collaboration, feedback, and visibility into deliverables |
| Webserv PPC AM (optional) | Joins if there's overlap on shared campaigns, landing pages, or cross-channel efforts |

> Associate Directors and the Director of SEO should NOT attend bi-weeklies unless the client is in a risk position. AMs own this meeting end-to-end.

---

## Meeting Prep — What You're Creating

1. A **one-line KPI insight** → Opens your pre-read email and Google doc agenda; anchors the performance story
2. A **short Deliverables + Quick Wins table** → Summarizes what happened, why it matters, and what's next
3. A few **Relationship & Alignment prompts** → Guides your conversation and helps surface opportunities or risks
4. A finalized **pre-read email draft** → Sent to the client 1 business day before the meeting; serves as your outline during the call
5. **Post-call recap email** → Created after the meeting using the transcript prompt to confirm decisions, next steps, and client action items

> **No slide deck is required.** Paste the pre-read email in your ongoing Google Doc agenda and use it as your talking guide, share your screen from the Google Doc only. Keep the call conversational.

**Trusted Advisor Reminder**: You're not reporting data, you're connecting outcomes to strategy. Use each touchpoint to show progress, simplify decisions, and strengthen the relationship.

---

## Agenda Template

**Purpose**: To review recent SEO progress, share quick wins, and align on upcoming priorities that support your business goals.

1. **Performance Pulse & Key Insights**
   - Quick overview of how organic traffic and lead pacing are trending
   - What's driving movement or why performance shifted

2. **Progress & Quick Wins**
   - Review key updates and deliverables from the past two weeks
   - Highlight what's working well and how it supports larger objectives
   - Share what's coming next and any dependencies

3. **Partnership & Alignment**
   - Open discussion: feedback, lead quality, new initiatives, or observations
   - Confirm next steps, responsibilities, and upcoming deliverables

**Goal**: To strengthen client trust and momentum by connecting SEO progress to business outcomes, highlighting what's working, and aligning on next priorities — all through a simple, conversational check-in that reinforces partnership and forward motion.

## How to Prep for Each Bi-Weekly

### Step 1: KPI Snapshot
- Open the Looker Studio SEO NSM: Organic Sessions Dashboard report and CallRail/CTM dashboard
- Pull current Organic Sessions and VOBs (if available) for QTD
- Enter these into the shared SEO NSM Tracker Google Sheet — this auto-calculates pacing (On Track / Behind / Ahead)
- Add one line of context or insight:
  > "Organic sessions pacing slightly behind due to lower mid-funnel search volume post-October peak."

> **Trusted-advisor tip**: Always connect the number to a reason or learning — never just report data.

---

### Step 2: Deliverables + Quick Wins
Combine project tracking and quick-win storytelling in one pass.
- Review Asana or Airtable for progress this quarter
- For each core area, note both "what happened" and "why it matters"

| Area | What We Did / Learned | What's Next |
|------|-----------------------|-------------|
| New & Updated Content | Published 2 new pages ("Outpatient IOP," "Adderall Withdrawal") → early signs of ranking traction for Dallas queries | Update modality pages for local targeting |
| Technical Maintenance | Fixed 404s + redirects live → improved crawl efficiency | Sitemap validation post-deploy |
| CRO / UX | Tested new hero CTA → +18% CTR, leads trending up | Form UX updates on content hub template |
| Local SEO / GBP | Posted 4 GBP updates, 2 Q&As, and received 3 new reviews | Client to respond to reviews + continue Jumper campaign |

---

### Step 3: Relationship & Alignment Pulse

This part is about reading the room, emotionally and strategically. The goal isn't to sell: it's to listen, align, and find opportunities where the client's needs intersect with what Webserv can deliver next.

**Client Relationship Pulse Prompts**:

| What to Listen For | What to Prompt the Client With | What It Might Indicate | What to Do Next |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| "I haven't had time to look at the reports" or "I just trust you guys" | "How's everything been feeling on your side lately — are we making it easy for your team to stay in the loop?" | Trust is high, but visibility is low — risk long-term if leadership changes | Reinforce trust with a concise KPI summary in next pre-read. Flag for AD if pattern continues |
| Mentions of new offerings, rebrands, campaigns | "Are there any new initiatives or services your leadership is focusing on this quarter?" | Signals timing for expanding SEO scope or aligning with upcoming launches | Note all new initiatives. Identify needed landing pages, schema, or GBP updates. Flag for AD |
| Concerns about lead type or drop-offs | "Have there been any changes to lead quality or volume lately?" | Opens the door to CRO/paid media support | Ask clarifying questions ("Are those leads coming from organic or maps?"). Flag to AD for cross-sell opportunity review |
