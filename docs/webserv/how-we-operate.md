# How We Operate (READ FIRST)

> Purpose: This page defines how SEO is practiced at Webserv.
> This is not a task list. This is how we think, act, and show up.

---

## Our SEO Philosophy

### What We Believe
- SEO is a business growth channel, not a traffic exercise
- Rankings are indicators — admissions are the goal
- Google rewards clarity, credibility, and consistency, not hacks
- Behavioral health SEO requires higher trust, higher scrutiny, and higher standards
- SEO, CRO, Technical, Local, and Content cannot operate in silos

### What This Means in Practice
- We prioritize impact over volume
- We avoid tactics we wouldn't defend to Google or a client
- We build for long-term equity, not short-term wins
- We explain why, not just what

---

## How Decisions Are Made

### Decision Hierarchy
1. Client business goals
2. Search intent & user need
3. Google quality & compliance standards
4. Historical performance data
5. SEO best practices

> If a tactic violates any of the above, we don't do it.

### Default Decision Principles
- When in doubt, simplify
- When traffic is flat, improve relevance before adding volume
- When conversions drop, fix experience before creating content
- When volatility hits, pause, diagnose, then act

## Roles & Accountability

### Clear Ownership
- **Director / AD**: Strategy quality, risk oversight, escalation
- **SEO AM**: Execution, client communication, performance monitoring
- **Cross-functional**: CRO, Paid, Dev alignment when needed

### What "Ownership" Means
- You don't just report problems — you bring options
- You don't wait for perfect data — you make informed calls
- You don't hide behind tools — you interpret them

---

## How We Work With Clients

### Expectations We Set
- SEO is iterative, not instant
- Not every month looks the same
- Short-term volatility ≠ long-term failure
- Transparency > false certainty

### How We Communicate
- Clear, calm, and confident
- No jargon without explanation
- No panic during algorithm updates
- No overpromising — ever

### Our Client Red Lines
- No deceptive claims
- No unethical tactics
- No pressure to "game" Google
- No shortcuts that risk YMYL trust

---

## Quality Standards (Non-Negotiables)

### Every SEO Initiative Must:
- Align with search intent
- Meet EEAT expectations
- Be defensible to SEO Leadership
- Be defensible in a Google review
- Be explainable to a client
- Be measurable beyond rankings

> If it doesn't meet these: It doesn't ship.

---

## How We Handle Change & Volatility

### Algorithm Updates
- We observe before reacting
- We diagnose with data, not fear
- We communicate early and calmly
- We document learnings for future use

### When Performance Drops
- We triage before pivoting
- We isolate variables
- We avoid mass changes without evidence
- We escalate early when needed

### Expectations
- Strategies are documented
