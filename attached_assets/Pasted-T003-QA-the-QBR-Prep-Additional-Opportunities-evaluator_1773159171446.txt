T003 — QA the QBR Prep Additional Opportunities evaluator with mocked report states

Objective

Do not redesign the feature.
Do not change the UI.
Do not change the AdditionalOpportunity output schema.

Your job is to pressure-test the current Additional Opportunities evaluator in server/qbrPrepSectionGenerator.ts using mocked QBR Prep report states and verify that the logic behaves correctly across positive, negative, and edge-case scenarios.

What to test

Run the current generateAdditionalOpportunities() logic against at least 10 mocked report states that cover both valid and invalid recommendation cases.

The goal is to verify:
- false positives do not appear
- valid opportunities do appear
- standard-scope issues stay out of Additional Opportunities
- upsell vs cross-sell classification is correct
- top-1-to-3 ranking works correctly
- threshold and gating behave as intended
- phrasing does not overclaim what the report evidence actually proves

Create mocked report states for at least these 10 cases:

CASE 1 — Weak account, on pace, weak evidence
Expected result:
- no additional opportunities
Purpose:
- verifies weak / on-pace cases stay suppressed

Expected result:
- Authority-Building upsell appears
Purpose:
- verifies Signal A fires only when real demand and business pressure exist

Expected result:
- no Authority-Building upsell
Purpose:

- ranking order is sensible
- weaker candidates are suppressed when stronger ones exist

Important QA rules

1. Do not just print whether a signal fired
Show the reasoning chain.

2. Do not overclaim demand evidence
Especially for the broader scan / Signal F case:
- if there are zero confirmed high-intent organic clusters, the recommendation wording must NOT claim confirmed treatment-intent demand from SEO data
- it may frame paid search as a way to test and capture priority demand faster
- it may not imply the report proved organic treatment-intent demand when it did not

3. Standard-scope issues must stay out
Examples that should usually remain standard scope:
- local SEO cleanup
- CTR optimization
- metadata improvements
- technical cleanup
- CTA/internal-link fixes
- content refreshes
- normal geo-page improvements
- normal blog-to-service journey fixes

4. Tracking gaps must stay out
Missing conversion tracking must not appear as an Additional Opportunity unless there is already explicit logic for a distinct packaged paid CRO / analytics project, which currently does not exist.
So the expected behavior in testing is that tracking gaps remain excluded.

5. No filler
If a mocked case is weak, the correct result is no opportunity.
