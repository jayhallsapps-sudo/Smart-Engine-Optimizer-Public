Never invent data.

If a required value cannot be pulled confidently, leave it blank, use an em dash, or use Manual entry needed.

Prefer first-party source data over third-party estimates.

Treatment Center SEO Priority Framework
Tier 1 — Trust & Eligibility

Highest priority.

Covers pages that define how someone gets help.

Includes:

Detox

Residential / inpatient

PHP / IOP

Dual diagnosis

Verify insurance

Contact / admissions

Prioritize when:

a core service page is missing

service intent is split across multiple URLs

admissions path is unclear

high-intent users are routed poorly

Content scope:

new Tier 1 page = 2 credits

refresh Tier 1 page = usually 1 credit

light CRO-only update on an existing Tier 1 page = 0 credit if under 1 hour

Tier 2 — Structural Authority

Second priority.

Covers structural hub pages that help Google understand the site.

Includes:

Conditions hub

Therapies hub

Primary location page

Prioritize when:

site structure is weak

internal authority flow is unclear

hubs are missing

Content scope:

new structural page = 2 credits

refresh = usually 1 credit

Tier 3 — Consolidation & Cleanup

Third priority.

Covers URL sprawl, internal competition, and legacy SEO drag.

Includes:

duplicate service pages

near-me / location overlap

thin archive pages

overlapping keyword-variant pages

Usually technical / strategy work, not content-credit work.

Generally outside content credits and should be handled as technical cleanup.

Tier 4 — Conversion & Differentiation

Fourth priority.

Covers pages that influence choice once the core offer is already clear.

Includes:

About

Clinical leadership

Staff

Aftercare

Alumni

Content scope:

new page = 2 credits

refresh = usually 1 credit

light CRO polish = 0 credit if minor

Tier 5 — Expansion & Demand Capture

Lowest priority.

Covers blogs, FAQs, informational content, and long-tail demand capture.

Only prioritize when Tier 1–3 issues are mostly resolved.

Content scope:

new blog / informational page = 1 credit

major long-form strategic asset = 2 credits in rare cases

standard refresh = 1 credit

lighter optimization = 0.5 credit

Content credit rules

New service or signature content = 2 credits.

New standard blog content = 1 credit.

Tier 1–3 refreshes are generally 1 full credit.

Tier 4–5 refreshes are generally 0.5 credit.

Tier 1–3 refreshes can be 0.5 credit only when the page is already well built and only needs a lighter copy-only update.

Technical updates are outside the content credit system.

How to choose new content topics

Do not choose topics randomly.

Choose topics using this priority:

non-brand queries with meaningful impressions but low or zero clicks in GSC

topics tied to core services, admits, VOBs, or qualified calls

topics that support Tier 1 or Tier 2 pages through internal linking

topics competitors rank for that the client should credibly cover

topics that fill a real topical gap, not just a volume opportunity

Avoid choosing topics based only on raw search volume.

Prefer topics where:

intent is relevant

the site has a realistic chance to compete

the content can support service-page authority or conversion paths

Do not recommend new blog content when Tier 1–3 problems are still the main blocker.

Use GSC first for topic discovery.

Use GA4 to validate whether related content actually supports conversions.

Use SEMrush / Ahrefs only as backup validation for competitor opportunities and keyword gaps.
