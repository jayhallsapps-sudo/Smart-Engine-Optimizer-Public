Fix 4 — Scheduler concurrency lock

Prevent two ticks from running the same schedule simultaneously. Two acceptable approaches:

In-memory Set<scheduleId> of currently-running IDs — fast, but doesn't help across restarts.
DB-level running_started_at timestamp on the report_schedules row, set before the run starts, cleared after. A tick skips any schedule with a non-null running_started_at younger than 10 minutes.
Pick the second one — it's slightly more work but survives restarts and races. Report which approach you used.
