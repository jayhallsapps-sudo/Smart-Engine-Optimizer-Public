# SOP: Verify Insurance Page

Part of Care Access Tier 1: Treatment Center SEO Priority Framework

---

## 1. URL, Indexing & Page Placement

### URL Structure
- `/verify-insurance` (preferred)
- `/insurance-verification` (acceptable alternative)

**Rules**:
- Single canonical URL
- No parameters
- No location-stacking in URL

### Page Title (SERP Title Guidance)
Clear, trust-first, non-promotional.

**Format**: `Verify Insurance for Mental Health & Addiction Treatment | [Brand Name]`

**Rules**: No urgency language, no guarantees, no pricing claims

### Meta Description Guidance
Reassurance-focused, not sales-driven.

**Example**: "Verify your insurance coverage for mental health or addiction treatment. Confidential, no obligation, and reviewed by our admissions team."

**Rules**: Avoid "free," "guaranteed," or "instant approval" — focus on safety + process

---

## 2. Navigation & Internal Linking Rules

### Top-Level Navigation
- YES, this page belongs in primary navigation
- Label exactly: **"Verify Insurance"**
- Do NOT nest under "Admissions"
- Do NOT rename to "Check Coverage" or "Insurance Help"
- This is a high-intent, utility page

### Footer Navigation
Also linked in footer under: Admissions, Insurance, Get Help

### Pages That MUST Link to Verify Insurance
- Homepage (hero secondary CTA)
- Admissions page
- Program pages (PHP, IOP, Residential, etc.)
- Conditions pages (depression, anxiety, substance use)
- Contact page

**Rules**: Anchor text should be "Verify Insurance" — no euphemisms or variations

---

## 3. Page Structure

### SECTION 1: Hero / Orientation Block (No CTA)
**Purpose**: Reassure + frame the form.

**Must Include**:
- H1: "Verify Your Insurance for Mental Health & Addiction Treatment"
- Subheadline: "Confidential. No obligation. Takes about 2 minutes."
- Insurance logos (6–12 real carriers, placed between headline and form, only carriers actually accepted)
- Trust microcopy: "HIPAA-compliant" and "Your information is private"

**NOT allowed**: No buttons, no phone number, no links

---

### SECTION 2: Insurance Verification Form
**Purpose**: This IS the CTA.

**Placement**: Immediately visible above the fold. No scrolling required on desktop.

**Form Requirements**:
- Embedded on page
- 6–8 required fields maximum
- Required fields: First name, Phone number, Email, Insurance provider, Date of birth, Policyholder name (if different)
- Optional fields clearly labeled
- No CAPTCHA unless legally required

**Submit Button**: "Verify My Insurance" — only primary CTA on page

**Microcopy (Under Button)**: "We'll contact you within 24 hours to review your benefits."

**Post-Submission Confirmation**: Explains next steps, sets response timeframe, reiterates confidentiality

---

### SECTION 3: Immediate Trust Reinforcement
**Purpose**: Reduce post-form anxiety.

**Must Include**:
- Short reassurance block: "Our admissions team works directly with your insurance provider so you don't have to."
- HIPAA/privacy reminder
- No outbound links

---

### SECTION 4: How Insurance Verification Works
**Purpose**: Remove uncertainty.

**Structure**: 3-step explanation:
1. You submit your information
2. We verify benefits with your insurance provider
3. We contact you to review your options

**Rules**: Plain language only, no billing jargon, emphasize advocacy not process

---

### SECTION 5: Conditions & Programs (Context Only)
**Purpose**: Self-identification and reassurance.

**Must Include**:
- Brief conditions list: Anxiety, depression, trauma, substance use, etc.
- Brief program types: Residential, PHP, IOP, Outpatient (as applicable)
- Framing: "Many insurance plans cover these levels of care."

**Rules**: No outcomes, no superiority claims, no medical promises

---

### SECTION 6: Social Proof
**Purpose**: Normalize action.

**Requirements**:
- 2–4 testimonials
- Focus on: ease of insurance process, feeling supported, professionalism
- First name + state only (or anonymous)
- No clinical claims
- Optional soft reminder CTA: Inline text "You can verify your insurance above." (anchor link)

---

### SECTION 7: Who Will Review Your Information (E-E-A-T)
**Purpose**: Demonstrate real humans and accountability.

**Must Include**:
- Admissions team framing (not sales)
- Mention licensed clinicians involved
- Credentials listed (LCSW, LMFT, MD, etc.)
- Optional: headshots, first name + title only, link to team profiles

**Tone**: Calm, professional, supportive

---

### SECTION 8: Secondary Contact Options
**Purpose**: Capture non-form users without stealing focus.

**Allowed**:
- "Prefer to talk? Call our admissions team."
- Optional chat option

**Rules**: Smaller size, secondary color, never placed above the form

---

### SECTION 9: Compliance & Safety Disclaimers

**Must Include**:
- Coverage disclaimer
- Emergency disclaimer: "If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room."
- HIPAA/privacy statement
- Link to privacy policy

---

### SECTION 10: Footer-Level Authority & Local Signals

**Must Include**:
- City + state reference + map
- State licensure language (if applicable)
- "Serving patients in [state(s)]"
