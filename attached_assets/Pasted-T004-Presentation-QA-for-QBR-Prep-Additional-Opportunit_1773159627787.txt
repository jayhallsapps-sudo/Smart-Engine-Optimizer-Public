T004 — Presentation QA for QBR Prep Additional Opportunities

Objective

Do not change the classification logic unless a presentation issue reveals a real output defect.
Do not change the UI structure unless absolutely necessary.

Your job is to review how Additional Opportunities actually read in the QBR Prep preview and print/export output, now that T003 confirmed the evaluator is production-safe.

Focus areas

1. Card readability in context
Check how 1, 2, and 3 opportunity cards read when rendered together in:
- qbr-prep preview
- qbr-prep print/export view

2. Wording quality
Review titles, why_now lines, evidence bullets, recommendations, and framing lines for:
- repetition
- robotic phrasing
- overuse of “accelerate” / “current quarter” / similar stock phrases
- wording that sounds too salesy
- wording that overstates certainty
- wording that sounds redundant when multiple cards appear together

3. Visual hierarchy
Confirm that when cards render:
- titles are easy to scan
- evidence is clearly separated from recommendation
- framing reads like optional advisory language, not the core pitch
- cards do not visually compete with the manual AddableReportTable below them

4. Print cleanliness
Confirm the print/export view:
- does not create awkward page breaks inside cards if avoidable
- preserves readable spacing
- does not duplicate or conflict with manual rows
- looks intentional when 0, 1, 2, or 3 opportunities are present

5. Empty-state behavior
Confirm:
- if no auto opportunities exist and no manual rows exist, Section 8 stays hidden in print
- preview behavior remains correct
- no awkward empty headings or spacing appear

What to test

Use at least these render scenarios:

A. No opportunities
B. One upsell card
C. One cross-sell card
D. Two cards with clearly different types
E. Three cards together
F. Three cards where wording could become repetitive if poorly written
G. Auto-generated cards plus manual AM rows below
H. Manual rows only, no auto cards

What to review specifically

- Are any titles too long or too generic?
- Do any why_now sentences sound inflated?
- Do any evidence bullets repeat the same idea in slightly different words?
- Do recommendation lines feel concrete?
- Do framing lines sound optional and advisor-led?
- When 3 cards appear, do they still feel distinct?
- Does the section feel like consultative guidance rather than a sales block?

Allowed improvements

You may make small wording-template refinements if needed, such as:
- tightening repetitive phrasing
- shortening titles
- improving evidence bullet clarity
- reducing repeated “if the client wants to move faster” style wording
- making framing lines more natural

Do not alter the core evaluator or scoring unless a presentation issue proves a real output flaw.

Deliverables

Report back with:
1. exact file(s) changed
2. whether logic changed or only presentation wording/templates
3. the render scenarios tested
4. any wording issues found
5. any fixes applied
6. confirmation that preview and print both look clean
7. confirmation that empty-state behavior remains correct
8. 3 before/after examples if you refined wording
9. final verdict on whether the section is now ready for live AM use
