# SOP: Updated Blog Content (Content Refresh)

**Purpose**: Improve performance, accuracy, and search alignment of existing blog content through structured updates, research, and on-page optimization.

**Scope**: Applies to already-published blog articles that require SEO improvements.

---

## Process

### Step 1: Page Review & Baseline
- Open the live article
- Identify:
  - Current rank for target keyword for last 28 days (in Google Search Console)
  - Weak or unclear intro
  - Missing or weak CTA
  - Thin sections or gaps
  - Missing data, stats, or sources
  - Poor internal linking

---

### Step 2: Research & SERP Validation (Required)
- Review the top 3 non-Wikipedia SERP results for the target keyword
  - Reference SERP via Ahrefs Keyword Overview
- Confirm:
  - All major subtopics are covered
  - No user-intent gaps (e.g. cost, eligibility, risks, FAQs)
- Research and validate:
  - Updated stats or data points
  - Government, academic, or peer-reviewed sources (no competitors)

---

### Step 3: Update Content
- Update or rewrite sections as needed using the client's Claude project
- First sentence must follow this format (semantic triple):
  > **[KEYWORD] is … [clear, concise 1-sentence definition]**
- Ensure:
  - CTA exists above the fold
  - Heading structure (H2/H3) is logical and complete
- Add links:
  - **External links**: 1–2 authoritative sources supporting factual claims
  - **Internal links**:
    - Ensure 3+ internal links
    - First internal link should support the primary service tied to the content

---

### Step 4: On-Page QA
Verify on the live page:
- CTA above the fold
- Heading structure makes sense and is properly formatted
- Image includes descriptive, keyword-aligned alt text
- SEO title and meta description:
  - Present
  - Accurate
  - Not truncated
- All internal and external links resolve correctly

> **Fix all issues before proceeding.**

---

### Step 5: Post Update
- Add links to this updated article from **3–6 relevant existing pages**
- Use contextual, non-generic anchor text

---

### Step 6: Finalize & Log

**Part 1: In Airtable**
- Update status to **Live**
- Update Published Date
- Remove Due Date
- In the record's AT Notes section drop in the following:
  - Your initials & the date, the target keyword and its current rank (if applicable)
  - A bulleted list of what you updated (5–10 points max)

**Example Airtable Note**:
> CB - Jan 19/26, attachment based therapy, rank 16
> - Answered the keyword intent in the first sentence
> - CTA above the fold
> - Mirrored H2/H3 structure
> - Keyword used in more headings
> - FAQ Refinement

**Part 2: In the client's Slack channel**
Post updated URL in the client Slack channel with the same list of the edits you made on the record.
