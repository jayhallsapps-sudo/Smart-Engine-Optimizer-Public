# SEO Account Manager Job Description

## Role Overview

The SEO Account Manager is a trusted advisor and strategic partner to their clients. They drive measurable growth through strong SEO strategy, clear communication, and a focus on business outcomes.

This role requires both technical expertise and strategic thinking. AMs plan and lead all SEO work, including AI-generated content creation and optimization, E-E-A-T review, technical audits, and conversion improvements. They manage their accounts with independence, taking full ownership of strategy, execution, and performance outcomes.

**This role reports to their SEO Associate Director.**

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Strategic SEO Ownership
- Owns end-to-end SEO strategy and performance outcomes for assigned clients
- Conducts full audits (technical, content, authority) and translates findings into prioritized, actionable roadmaps
- Aligns strategies with client business goals (e.g., conversions, patient acquisition, lead quality)
- Adapts to Google algorithm changes, AI-driven search evolution, and shifting user behavior
- Ensures all recommendations are data-backed, documented, and tied to measurable impact

### 2. Performance Management & Insights
- Tracks traffic, keyword visibility, conversions, and engagement against KPIs
- Produces monthly and quarterly reports with clear "so what / now what" insights
- Uses data to forecast growth, identify risks, and adjust strategy accordingly
- Defines and reports on North Star Metrics (NSMs) that link SEO performance to business outcomes

### 3. Content, E-E-A-T & CRO Integration
- Owns content and conversion strategy — ensuring every page supports visibility, user trust, and business impact
- Oversees AI-generated content for accuracy, tone, and intent alignment, applying E-E-A-T principles
- Incorporates SME quotes or client insights to reinforce topical authority
- Designs and executes CRO strategies — analyzing user behavior to optimize conversion paths, CTAs, and UX flow
- Implements smaller CRO and on-page improvements directly; partners with Dev only for major site changes
- Connects content and CRO improvements to measurable outcomes (lead quality, conversions, revenue impact)

### 4. Technical SEO & Quality Assurance
- Owns technical SEO strategy and implementation for crawlability, indexation, and performance
- Conducts regular audits (site structure, Core Web Vitals, metadata, schema, internal linking)
- Executes or oversees fixes directly; engages Dev only for structural updates or redesigns
- Uses tools (Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, GA4, GSC) to track health and validate implementations
- QAs all technical and CRO updates post-launch and clearly communicates business impact

### 5. Client & Account Management
- Serves as the primary client lead — managing communication, strategy, and relationship health
- Leads regular check-ins and quarterly business reviews (QBRs), tying SEO outcomes to business results
- Manages expectations proactively, communicates timelines and priorities clearly, and escalates only when needed
- Identifies and pitches consultative growth opportunities (upsell or cross-sell) when strategically aligned
- Documents all client communications, deliverables, and insights
- Maintains high client satisfaction and retention through proactive partnership and transparency

### 6. Collaboration & Internal Contribution
- Acts as strategic owner across all accounts, managing SEO, CRO, and light technical work independently
- Engages with PPC, Sales, or Admissions Ops only for growth initiatives or multi-channel opportunities
- Partners with Dev for large-scale projects (new builds, redesigns), ensuring SEO and CRO accuracy
- Shares learnings, SOPs, and case studies to improve team performance and alignment
- Participates in internal discussions, training, and mentorship

## Competencies & Skill Framework

### 1. Channel & Technical Expertise
- Advanced SEO knowledge across technical, on-page, and content strategy
- Conducts independent SEO audits identifying both technical and strategic priorities
- Connects keyword and content opportunities to conversion and patient acquisition journeys
- Utilizes core tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Screaming Frog, GA4, GSC, Looker Studio)
- Builds content frameworks and internal linking strategies that reinforce topical authority and funnel intent

**Indicators of Growth**:
- Developing: Requires oversight to complete audits or apply technical insights
- Proficient: Independently builds SEO strategies aligned with business KPIs
- Advanced: Anticipates technical or content issues before they impact performance; mentors peers

### 2. Strategy & Analytical Thinking
- Builds integrated, goal-driven SEO strategies tied to client business outcomes
- Defines and tracks NSMs that connect SEO performance to conversions, leads, or revenue
- Uses forecasting to set realistic growth goals and interpret impact
- Turns data into insights and insights into strategy adjustments

**Indicators of Growth**:
- Developing: Relies on templates and reactive thinking
- Proficient: Proactively connects data to business goals and communicates it confidently
- Advanced: Shapes long-term strategy and forecasting models across multiple accounts

### 3. Client & Consultative Skills
- Leads client relationships with ownership, clarity, and composure
- Sets and resets expectations effectively; uses structured objection handling ("Acknowledge → Clarify → Reframe → Reconfirm")
- Demonstrates consultative selling — identifies growth opportunities, proposes initiatives, and frames recommendations around ROI
- Tells cohesive performance stories linking SEO metrics to real-world business impact

**Indicators of Growth**:
- Developing: Provides updates but lacks strategic framing or confidence
- Proficient: Consistently communicates insights and handles standard objections
- Advanced: Trusted advisor status; drives strategic alignment and influences client direction

### 4. Project & Account Management
- Plans and manages client campaigns with structure, clarity, and accountability
- Uses project management systems (Asana, Notion, ClickUp) to organize tasks, dependencies, and timelines
- Ensures deliverables meet deadlines and quality standards without leadership intervention
- Communicates proactively about risks, blockers, and outcomes

**Indicators of Growth**:
- Developing: Reactive; requires oversight for organization or prioritization
- Proficient: Manages full campaigns independently; maintains visibility and momentum
- Advanced: Improves workflows, mentors peers, and contributes to operational best practices

### 5. Leadership, Team, & Culture
- Models professionalism, curiosity, and proactive communication
- Contributes positively to team culture — shares learnings, supports peers, and engages constructively in feedback loops
- Demonstrates cross-department collaboration with PPC and other operational teams when appropriate
- Participates in department development: process improvements, SOPs, or documentation

### 6. Growth Mindset & Self-Development
- Stays informed on Google algorithm updates, AI search evolution, and best practices in SEO
- Actively applies new learnings to client work and shares insights with peers
- Seeks out feedback, owns growth areas, and demonstrates measurable improvement over time
- Contributes to team development by sharing case studies, insights, or process improvements

---

## Role Level & Growth Path

- **Senior SEO Account Manager** (larger, higher-revenue clients)
- **SEO Associate Director** (team leadership and advanced strategic oversight)
