## Key Principles (Quick Reference)

1. **SEO is a business growth channel, not a traffic exercise.** Rankings are indicators — admissions are the goal.
2. **Behavioral health SEO is YMYL.** Higher trust, higher scrutiny, higher standards.
3. **Decision hierarchy**: Client business goals → Search intent → Google quality → Data → Best practices.
4. **Local SEO rule**: No physical address = no location page. Proximity is not a location.
5. **Reporting philosophy**: Connect outcomes to strategy. Never just report data.
6. **Quality standard**: Every SEO initiative must be defensible to Google, to leadership, and explainable to a client.
7. **Documentation**: If it isn't documented, it didn't happen.

## Industry Context for SmartEO

SmartEO serves **behavioral health treatment centers** — addiction treatment, mental health facilities, and dual-diagnosis programs. Key context:

- Primary conversion goal: **VOBs (Verified Organic Benefits/Leads)** and **Admissions calls**
- Primary content risk: **YMYL classification** — Google holds these pages to a higher standard
- Insurance landscape: Most clients prefer **PPO/out-of-network** coverage; **LegitScript certification** required for paid advertising
- Key compliance: **HIPAA** applies to all patient data handling; **no retargeting** for addiction treatment
- Reporting metrics: Organic Sessions, VOBs, Admissions calls, Keyword visibility by cluster, Conversion page performance
