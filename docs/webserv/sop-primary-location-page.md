# SOP: Primary Location Page

> Read first: Local SEO for Behavioral Health 101 (`local-seo-behavioral-health.md`)

---

## Before You Build: Eligibility Check

Before creating or approving a Primary Location Page, confirm:
- There is a real, physical treatment facility
- The address can be publicly listed
- Care is actually delivered at this location
- There is no existing Primary Location Page for this address

> **If the location does not have an address → do not create a page.**

---

## 1. URL, Indexing & Page Placement

### URL Structure
- `/locations/[city-or-area]/` (preferred)
- One URL per real, physical address

**Rules**:
- Single canonical URL
- No parameters
- No service terms in URL
- No "rehab-in-city" or keyword-stuffed patterns

### Indexing Rules
- Page is indexable
- Canonical points to itself
- Not blocked by robots.txt
- Not redirected from other geo variants

### Page Placement
- Lives under `/locations/`
- Linked from a Locations index page (if multiple locations exist)
- Do NOT place under `/services/`
- Do NOT surface as a primary nav item unless multiple locations exist

---

## 2. Navigation & Internal Linking Rules

### Top-Level Navigation
- Linked under "Locations" or "Our Locations"
- Do NOT list cities individually in the main nav
- Do NOT treat as a conversion-first nav item

### Pages That MUST Link to This Location Page
- Homepage (if single location)
- Locations index page
- Relevant service pages (Residential, Detox, PHP, etc.)
- Contact / Admissions page

### Location Page Links Out To
- Tier 1 service pages offered at this facility
- Contact / Admissions
- Directions (map link)

**Rules**:
- No links to city-based SEO pages
- No excessive links to other locations

---

## 3. Page Structure

### SECTION 1: Hero / Orientation Block
**Purpose**: Immediately establish legitimacy and location clarity.

**Must Include**:
- H1: Facility name + location
- Short subheadline describing the facility
- City + state clearly visible
- Optional: exterior or facility image

**Rules**: No marketing hype, no keyword stuffing, no city-ranking language

---

### SECTION 2: Address & Contact Information (Critical)
**Purpose**: Remove doubt about whether this is a real place.

**Must Include**:
- Full physical address (NAP consistent)
- Phone number
- Embedded map
- "Get Directions" link

**Placement Rules**: Must appear high on the page, must be easily scannable

---

### SECTION 3: Location Overview
**Purpose**: Explain what this facility is and who it's for.

**Must Include**:
- Brief description of the facility
- Type(s) of care delivered at this address
- Who this location is best suited for
- Trust-first, informational tone

**Rules**: No outcome claims, no superiority language, no sales framing

---

### SECTION 4: Services Available at This Location
**Purpose**: Connect "where care happens" to "what care is offered."

**Must Include**:
- List of services actually available here
- Links to Tier 1 service pages
- Availability framing ("Available at this location")

**Rules**:
- Do NOT explain services in depth
- Do NOT list services not offered at this facility

---

### SECTION 5: Proximity & Service Area
**Purpose**: Acknowledge nearby demand without creating risk.

**Allowed**: One short paragraph mentioning nearby cities.

**Example**: "Located in [City A], serving clients from [Nearby City B] and surrounding areas."

**Rules**:
- No city subheadings
- No separate city sections
- No SEO-driven geo paragraphs

---

### SECTION 6: Trust & Legitimacy Signals
**Purpose**: Reinforce credibility in a regulated healthcare space.

**Must Include (at least one)**:
- Real facility photos
- Accreditations or licenses
- Staff or leadership mention
- Facility-specific testimonials (if available)

**Rules**: No stock-only imagery, no anonymous claims

---

### SECTION 7: Conversion Support (Secondary)
**Purpose**: Support action without competing with service pages.

**Allowed**:
- Call to Admissions
- Verify Insurance CTA
- Contact form link

**Rules**:
- Secondary emphasis only
- No aggressive or urgent language
- No multiple competing CTAs above the fold

---

### SECTION 8: Who Provides Care Here (E-E-A-T)
**Purpose**: Demonstrate real people and accountability.

**Must Include**:
- Admissions or clinical oversight mention
- Credentials where appropriate
- Optional: team photo or names

**Tone**: Professional, calm, trust-oriented

---

### SECTION 9: Compliance & Safety Disclaimers

**Must Include**:
- Licensure or regulatory language (if applicable)
- Emergency disclaimer
- Privacy / HIPAA reference

---

### SECTION 10: Footer-Level Local Signals

**Must Include**:
- City + state reference
- Map or address repeat
- "Serving patients in [state(s)]" language
