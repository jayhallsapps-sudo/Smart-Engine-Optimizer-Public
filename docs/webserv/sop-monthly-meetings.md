# SOP: SEO Monthly Meetings

**Purpose**: Monthly SEO meetings exist to connect performance trends to strategy, align on business objectives, and present forward-looking plans. Where bi-weeklies are about momentum and relationship, monthlies are about insight and direction — tying the past month's work to measurable impact and the next phase of growth.

---

## Meeting Specs

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Meeting Length | 45–60 minutes |
| Prep Time | ≤ 60 minutes |
| Responsible for Prep & Deck | Account Manager (AM) |
| Responsible for Emails & Insights | Associate Director (AD) |
| Informed | Director of SEO |
| Scheduling | Recurrent first week of the month |

> Try and never schedule meetings on Fridays to protect your working time.

**Meeting Title**: [Client Name] Webserv Monthly SEO Strategy Review

---

## Required Attendees

| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Associate Director (AD) | Leads strategic discussion, connects SEO to business impact |
| Account Manager (AM) | Provides tactical context and relationship continuity |
| Client POCs / Marketing Contact | Key decision-maker for SEO initiatives |
| PPC AM (optional) | Joins if PPC integration or shared campaigns |
| Director of SEO (optional) | Only for very specific clients Trevor has approved, or clients in a risk position (approved by Rebecca/ADs) |

## What You're Creating During Prep

1. **Executive KPI Summary** → Top-level performance vs. goal (Organic Sessions, VOBs, conversions)
2. **Performance Deep Dive** → What moved the needle: top gains, key learnings, challenges
3. **Strategic Initiatives Update** → Long-term project progress (content pillars, CRO, site structure)
4. **Next Month's Plan** → Strategic priorities, risks, and upcoming campaigns
5. **3–4 Slide Deck** → Simple, visual summary (details below)
6. **Post-Call Recap Email** → Confirm decisions, priorities, and key actions

> Keep the meeting visual, not verbose. The slides should guide the conversation, not read like a report.

**Trusted Advisor Reminder**: You're not showing data — you're showing meaning. Focus on what changed, why, and how you're adjusting strategy accordingly.

## Monthly Meeting Prep Checklist

### Step 1: Executive KPI Summary
**Pull From**: Looker Studio, CallRail/CTM (if available)

- Collect MoM and QoQ data for:
  - Organic Sessions (always)
  - Verified Organic Leads (VOBs) or Calls (if tracked)
  - Conversion rate or goal completions (if tracked)
- Identify KPI pacing vs. goal: On Track / Behind / Ahead
- Add one 2–3 sentence insight summary explaining what's driving movement

**Example**: "Organic sessions increased +10% MoM due to stronger visibility in IOP and detox clusters. Lead conversions steady — no drop in quality or volume."

**Output**: Slide 1 (Executive KPI Overview) + one-sentence summary to open the meeting.

> Trusted Advisor Tip: Frame the data like a business outcome, not a report. "Here's what's happening in search, and what it means for your pipeline."

---

### Step 2: Keyword Performance Analysis
**Pull From**: Google Search Console or SEMrush (Rank Tracker / Keyword Groups)

- Identify top 5 keyword clusters relevant to business goals (e.g., Detox, IOP, Outpatient)
- Capture MoM changes in: Average position, Impressions, Clicks
- Highlight top gaining and declining clusters
- Note any shifts in search intent (e.g., from informational → transactional)

**Example insight**: "IOP cluster gained +22% impressions and +2.4 avg. position — likely driven by stronger internal linking and recent blog coverage."

**Output**: Visual table or heat map (Slide 2) + 3 bullet insights for "What Moved the Needle"

> Trusted Advisor Tip: Tie clusters to conversions or future opportunities: "As visibility grows for IOP terms, this becomes a strong retargeting pool for PPC."

---

### Step 3: Conversion Page Performance
**Pull From**: Looker Studio, CallRail, or GA4 (Landing Page Report)

- Identify top 3–5 organic conversion-driving pages
- Note changes in traffic, conversions, and keyword sources
- Cross-reference performance with ranking movement (e.g., "Admissions page improved 5 positions, calls up 18%")
- Flag underperforming pages for CRO or content refresh

**Output**: Conversion highlights included on Slide 2 (as part of "What Moved the Needle") + 2–3 talking points for client discussion.

> Trusted Advisor Tip: "These three pages are driving 60% of calls, our goal is to expand that footprint."

---

### Step 4: Content Impact – 30-Day Lookback
**Pull From**: GSC (Page report), Google Analytics / Looker Studio or Airtable

- List new or refreshed pages published 30+ days ago
- Record performance snapshot: current position & impressions, clicks vs. prior month, whether any contributed to conversions
- Identify early traction and improvement opportunities

**Example insight**: "The 'Outpatient vs. Inpatient' guide published 6 weeks ago is now ranking #8 for 'Dallas IOP' — expected to reach top 5 next month."

**Output**: Table or quick visual integrated into Slide 3 or Slide 2 + talking point during "What Moved the Needle."

> Trusted Advisor Tip: Use this to connect output → performance → business value. This reinforces SEO as an investment, not purchasing deliverables.

---

### Step 5: Strategic Initiatives Update
**Pull From**: Asana quarterly project tracking

- Review current strategic initiatives:
  - Site architecture or technical cleanup
  - Content pillar builds
  - CRO testing / UX improvements
  - Local SEO / GBP optimization
- Mark each as Complete / In Progress / At Risk
- Add 1–2 bullets of measurable value or impact

**Example**: "Technical audit fixes reduced crawl errors by 24%, improving index coverage."

**Output**: Slide 3 (Strategic Initiatives)

> Trusted Advisor Tip: This section shows stewardship — emphasize consistency, not just performance spikes.

---

### Step 6: Next Month's Focus & Strategic Alignment

- Choose priorities based on data trends and business context:
  - Strengthen high-performing clusters
  - Optimize underperforming conversion pages
  - Continue CRO or local expansions
  - Plan new content aligned with business launches or seasonality
- Identify cross-functional dependencies (e.g., PPC, design, dev)
- Add 1 forward-looking opportunity (cross-sell or test)

**Output**: Slide 4 (Next Month's Focus)

> Trusted Advisor Tip: "We're doing this because it connects directly to your admissions growth goal."
